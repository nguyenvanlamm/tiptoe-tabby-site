Privacy Policy

Tiptoe Tabby · last updated 20 October 2026

Tiptoe Tabby is a skill game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the paw prints and best step count of each level, your yarn, boosters and outfits.
  • Your settings: the outfit your cat wears and whether the Steady tip has been shown.
  • Whether you have bought "All Rooms" and how many boosters you hold, plus the store's transaction ids so a purchase is never granted twice.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The Living Room (20 levels) is free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
