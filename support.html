How to play

  1. Drag anywhere to swing the back needle forward. Further drag, longer stride.
  2. Let go to put it down on something dry: a book, a cushion, the cat bed at the end.
  3. Lean too far or touch the floor and it is wet paws. Try again, or use a Rewind.

Walk a level in par steps or fewer to earn three paw prints. Rewind puts the cat back on its last stand after a fall; Steady slows time for six seconds.

Purchases

The Living Room (20 levels) is free. "All Rooms" is a one-time purchase that opens the Kitchen and the Laundry Room (40 more levels). The Booster Pouch and the Booster Basket add Rewinds and Steadies. Outfits are bought with yarn earned in the game, never with money. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Shop. All Rooms comes back; boosters are used up as you play, so they are not restored.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
