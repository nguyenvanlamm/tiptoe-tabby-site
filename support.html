How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to swing the cat's back knitting needle forward; the further you drag, the longer the stride. Let go to put the needle down on something dry: a book, a cushion, the cat bed at the end. If a needle touches the wet floor, or the cat leans too far, the walk ends and you try again. Walk a level in par steps or fewer to earn three paw prints. Rewind puts the cat back on its last stand after a fall; Steady slows time for six seconds.

Purchases

The Living Room (20 levels) is free. "All Rooms" is a one-time purchase that opens the Kitchen and the Laundry Room (40 more levels). The Booster Pouch and the Booster Basket add Rewinds and Steadies. Outfits are bought with yarn earned in the game, never with money. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
