Every step is a choice

Everything behaves

Books hold still. Cushions squash if you linger. Plates break after one step. The robot vacuum will not wait, and the dog does not stay asleep.

Short levels, real skill

60 levels across the Living Room, the Kitchen and the Laundry Room, most under a minute. Walk one in par steps to earn three paw prints.

Calm and fair

No ads, no account, no tracking. The Living Room is free; one purchase opens the other two rooms for good.

How to play

Drag anywhere to swing the back needle forward. Further drag, longer stride.

Let go to put it down on something dry: a book, a cushion, the cat bed at the end.

Lean too far or touch the floor and it is wet paws. Try again, or use a Rewind.

Questions

Is it free?

The Living Room, 20 levels, is free. "All Rooms" is a one-time purchase that opens the Kitchen and the Laundry Room, 40 more levels. Booster packs are optional; you also earn boosters and yarn by playing.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads of any kind.

What data does the game collect?

None. Your progress stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. You only need it to buy or restore a purchase.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, or use Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Shop. All Rooms comes back on any iPhone signed in with the same App Store account. Boosters are used up as you play, so they are not restored.

Which devices?

iPhone (iOS 15 or later).
